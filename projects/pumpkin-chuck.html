18th Annual Wright-Patterson Air Force Base EZ STEM Pumpkin Chuck

2023

Team ETHOS designed & built a torsion-powered catapult (onager) named The Phoenix. Sebastian can be seen launching a La Estrella pumpkin. He joined the team in 2023 to help build & assemble the machine.

The pumpkin Sebastian launched, happily named “The Empire Stripes Back”, flew 2,909 ft! The 8lb pumpkin experiences ~10,000 Gs when launching and hurls at an exit speed of ~440 mph!

Team ETHOS won 1st place in this competition with a max distance of 3,074 ft out of 5 throws! Back in 2019, the team achieved a world-record-breaking throw of 3,792 ft! Team ETHOS has appeared on the Science Channel’s “Punkin Chunkin”!
