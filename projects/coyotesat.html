Isomer Space Program

2021 - 2023

An ambitious idea led to an immensely rewarding journey. Sebastian joined Isomer Space Program shortly after inception to become the Lead Payload/Avionics Engineer.

After 2 years of research, grant seeking and testing, the team created CoyoteSat - the final design for the 1U CubeSat. The payload of the satellite is designed to detect gamma & X-ray particles in low-Earth orbit (LEO).
