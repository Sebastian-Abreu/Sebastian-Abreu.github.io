NYU Commencement Interview

2022

Sebastian was invited by NYU to be interviewed in front of 55,000 of his fellow NYU classmates, faculty & family during his Commencement Ceremony in May, 2022.

In it, he covered the major projects he helped lead during his time at NYU & future aspirations, including the making if a satellite with Isomer Space Program and rocketeering in the NASA USLI with Rogue Aerospace. You will also see Sebastian dancing with other exceptional classmates from the graduating class!
