Sebastian designed and manufactured a bipdeal robot for a semester-long project under Professor Hod Lipson’s Robtics Studio course. He programed the robot to walk with a top speed of 34 cm/s and is currently working to simulate walking gaits and implement machine learning code to train the robot how to walk faster. There are 2 major constraints that the robot design must abide to, which includes a $100 budget (excluding cost of motors, Raspberry Pi 4B, motor controller and battery) using up to 8 servo motors in total. With this in mind, Sebastian decided to make a digitigrade-like bipedal robot - taking inspiration from Cassie the robot from Agility Robotics. There are 4 servo motors in each leg (hip abduction, hip pitch, knee pitch and toe pitch). All structural components were 3D printed with PLA minus the four-bar linkage made for the shin - the parallel rods are carbon fiber tubes, chosen for their stiffness.
