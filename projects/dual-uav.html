Dual UAV Autonomous In-Air Takeoff & Docking System

2021 - 2022

The team designed, built & tested a world’s first - a vehicle capable of carrying multiple drones while taking off & docking mid-air. This ability is crucial to increase effectiveness and efficiency in search & rescue operations by covering the same area in a fraction of the time.

At the end of the 9-month project, the group, named Built Different, entered the NYU Tandon Capstone Project Competition to showcase their work. The project won 2 cash prize awards: Best Design Award Students’ Choice Award
