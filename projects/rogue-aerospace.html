Rogue Aerospace

2020 - 2022

Rogue Aerospace is a Vertically Integrated Project (VIP) team within the NYU Tandon School of Engineering. Sebastian was the Lead Mechanical Engineer of the team.

Rogue Aerospace was accepted for the first time into the 2021-2022 NASA University Student Launch Initiative (USLI), where they designed a rocket (left) that could reach 4, 900 feet into the sky!

RA won these awards at NASA USLI 2021: Overall Winner of the Design Division 1st Place Project Review Award 2nd Place Rookie Award

RA won these awards at NASA USLI 2022: 1st Place Vehicle Design Award, Presented by AIAA 1st Place Project Review Award 2nd Place Design Division
